[1943 - 2026]

Ghost builds computers.

For forty years, computers have worked the same way. You click, you type, you navigate through apps and menus and windows, and the machine does exactly what you tell it and nothing more. Every computer ever made has been a static machine waiting for its next instruction.

Now, with AI, computers will come alive. Every machine will have a ghost in it, an agent with full control of the computer at every level, from the hardware to the operating system to the software.

These agents will do incredible things. Yours will reason locally over every data point in your entire life: the emails in your inbox, the files in your drive, your calendar, your health data, your finances, the cameras you connect, the conversations you have. Your computer will work 24/7 as anything you ask it to be: your executive assistant, your health coach, your therapist.

Eventually, an entire ecosystem of devices will be built around these agents. They will see through the cameras you connect, control the lights and appliances in your home, and follow you through your day. Anything you want done, in software or in the world around you, will begin with a sentence. And the intelligence making it possible will be entirely your own.

AI will make your life unimaginably good. It all starts with computers.
